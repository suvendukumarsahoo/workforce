Processing applied (5 Aug 2026 session): filtered to Odisha's 30 districts,
simplified for web-map display (Douglas-Peucker, ~500m-1km tolerance via
@turf/simplify), and — for odisha-state.geojson only — dissolved into a
single outer boundary via @turf/union, keeping only the largest resulting
ring (the real state outline; ~200 sub-km2 fragments produced by the dissolve
were digitization/seam artifacts, not real geography, and were dropped).
